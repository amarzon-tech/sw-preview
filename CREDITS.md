# Image credits — Salt Whisperer v2 (v2-replay-pics)

Every food and plate photograph in this branch is an **original AI-generated image** created for Salt Whisperer. None were copied from Wikimedia, stock sites, or other apps.

- **Source:** Original AI-generated images, created for this educational app. Thirty-four images are reused from the `replay-pics` branch, where they were also original AI-generated work for the same app; the rest were generated for v2.
- **License:** Original work. Bundled locally so the app works offline. No third-party attribution is required.
- **Use:** Patient-education only. Images are illustrations of the named food; they are not photographs of a specific brand or product.

Generic, unbranded packaging is used where a can or bottle helps identify the food (for example regular vs low-sodium soy sauce). Labels are blank and carry no brand names. Compare cards use the photographs of the two foods they compare.
